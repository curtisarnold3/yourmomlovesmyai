STACKING THE GLASS…
AI Stack · 10 · The cake in 3D

Five panes of glass, and every string between them

Huang's line is that every app pulls on every layer beneath it, all the way down to the power plant. This is what that pull looks like. Each pane is one layer of the cake, each dot is a company or product, and each string is a real deal: a power contract, a chip order, a cloud lease, an equity stake, a model behind an app.

Click any dot, then hit Trace to the power plant. Start with Claude, Siri or Copilot and watch how much of the planet lights up.

The chip and neocloud strings come straight from this series' Sector Pulse and Contagion Monitor maps. The rest were researched for this page (as of Nov 18, 2026). Strings marked background weren't re-verified here.

Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · right-drag to pan · tap a string type to hide it
