Start with a dinner table

The $20 that paid for dinner four times

Four friends sit down for dinner with exactly one $20 bill between them. Ana hands it to Ben for a haircut. Ben hands it to Cy for a lasagna. Cy pays Dee for a logo. Dee pays Ana for a guitar lesson. Everybody just made a sale.

Now ask each of them how business is. Every single one will say: "I booked $20 this week." Four sales, four happy bookkeepers. And the only money that ever existed was still sitting in one hand at the end, exactly where it started.

Nobody lied. Each sale was real, each service was done, each receipt is honest. But if you add up the receipts and call it "demand," you've counted one bill over and over. That's the idea behind this entire episode.

Sales are not the same thing as money from outside the table.
