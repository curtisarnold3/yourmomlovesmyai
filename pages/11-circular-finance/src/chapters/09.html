Chapter 9 · The stress test

What if payments slow down?

Picture a row of dominoes where every domino is made of the same wood. Knock one over and you learn little. Knock them over in a different order and you learn who lands on whom. That's all this chapter does. We take one small cluster from our toy loop (a neocloud, meaning a newer company that rents out racks of AI chips, plus its lenders), squeeze it a few ways, and watch who still gets paid, who takes a haircut (the part of a payment they don't receive), and which promises get called. Then we follow the hit back to where the loop started: Nvidia's revenue.

What this tool does. You squeeze one small cluster four ways (less customer revenue, cheaper GPU rentals, higher interest rates, a lower Nvidia stock price) and it shows who gets paid first, who takes a loss, and what comes back to Nvidia. It opens on a mild preset, not a base case. Press “Reset to no stress” to see the cluster with nothing going wrong.

TOY MODEL: not a forecast. Not investment advice. One year, one cluster, $100 of seed cash. Every number below is hypothetical: it comes from knobs you set, not from anyone's books.
