Chapter 10 · The history rhyme

Is this 2000 again?

Around the turn of the millennium, the companies that made the internet's plumbing did something that sounds familiar. Lucent and Nortel sold their gear to a crowd of young phone and data carriers, and in many cases they helped those customers pay for it, through loans and guarantees. A couple of years later, a number of those customers were gone, and so was most of the vendors' stock value. People now line that story up next to Nvidia and the AI build-out. Is it the same movie? Let's put the facts side by side and see which parts match, which don't, and which we just can't tell yet.

A comparison, not a prediction. History rarely repeats on schedule. We show what's documented, tag how sure we are, and say “we don't know yet” where that's the truth. No verdict, and no suggestion that anyone today is doing what anyone did then.
