The Loop: Follow $100 Around the AI Money Loop

Who pays whom, with whose money, and how many times we count it.

Chipmakers invest in the cloud companies that buy their chips. Many of those are neoclouds, a neocloud being a newer company that rents out racks of AI chips. Neoclouds sign contracts with AI labs that the chipmakers also fund. It's not a scandal and it's not a free lunch. It's a loop, and the loop changes what "revenue" and "demand" mean. We follow one seed of cash around it, lap by lap, and tag every number with how much we trust it.

Toy model, per $100 of seed cash. Nothing here is a forecast, and none of it is investment advice. Promises are never counted as cash.
